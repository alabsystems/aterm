FinkHeavy
Copyright 1993-1996 Brand Design Co., Inc./House Industries. Font by Ken Barber.

FinkHeavy is the ACTUAL Animal Crossing typeface — Nintendo licensed it from
House Industries for the games' English releases (GameCube era through New
Leaf). Not a lookalike: this is the real face, version 001.000, as vendored by
Animal Crossing fan projects (retrieved from a GitHub mirror; the same bytes
circulate across the AC fan community).
