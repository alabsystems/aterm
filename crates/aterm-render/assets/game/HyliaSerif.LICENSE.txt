grant. The author's download page states the redistribution terms directly:

Embedding these bytes in the aterm binary IS redistribution. Contact the author
(https://artsyomni.com/hyliaserif/contact) and obtain their agreement before this
asset ships in a cut release. Until then it is a development-branch asset only.

Two further facts for whoever handles that conversation:

    pinned copy will drift from upstream — the exact concern the notice raises;
  * the face is a fan work evoking a Nintendo property. The other bundled game
    faces are deliberately open-licensed LOOKALIKES chosen to avoid that; this
    one is not, which is a different posture than `GAME_FONTS`' doc comment
    describes.
