## 0. Notation used in the statements

- `Words(s)` = `s.split_whitespace()`. `w[i]` is the i-th word.
- `Parts` / `p` = the request argv as `Vec<String>`. `p[0]` may be `@<sel>`. `forwarded_verb` / `forwarded_request` skip a leading `@sel`.
- `framing_of` = `aterm_types::control_verbs::framing_of` (`Lines | Bytes | Status`), the reply-framing table shared with the server.
- `render(b)` = `aterm_codec::tty::SafeStream` push(b) then finish: the terminal-safe rendering. It escapes active bytes as `\xNN` or `\u{..}` and keeps printable text and `\n`.
- `Probe` = the discovery verdict: `Answered(rows) | Refused{pid_running} | Denied | NoToken(path) | AuthRejected | Busy(line) | Foreign{token,facts} | Timeout | Other(io::Error)`.
- `DirOutcome` = `Found{path,n} | Empty | Missing{path,why} | Unreadable{path,err} | Unresolvable | Scoped{live_elsewhere}`.
- `Stamp` = `(boot: Option<BootClock>, wall: Option<SystemTime>)`. `Arrival` = `Fresh | Predates | Unknown | Shared`. `Hangup` = `Unknown | StillServing | Successor{path,pid} | Gone{waited,by_deadline}`.
- Exit codes: 0 success, 1 failure or empty fleet, 2 `EXIT_UNREACHABLE`, 75 `EXIT_REPLACED`, 124 `EXIT_TIMEOUT`.
- Effectful specs (sockets, fs, processes, threads) are stated over every peer behaviour or interleaving. Their pure cores are separate `contract` specs, so the pure half can be proved before gap 8 closes.
- Test ids are crate-relative: `src/lib_tests.rs::name` means `crates/aterm-ctl/src/lib_tests.rs`.
- Gap numbers are the rows of `docs/AUDIT-trust-for-test-migration-2026-10-15.md` §7.
